# Play Store listing: Ramayanam Wallpapers

**App name** (30 max): Ramayanam Wallpapers

**Short description** (80 max):
A Ramayana painting and its story as your wallpaper, a new one every day.

**Full description:**

Wake up to the Ramayana. Every day your phone wallpaper changes to the next incident of the epic: a classical painting in a gold frame, with the kanda, title and a short story of what happened.

HOW IT WORKS
• One incident a day, in order: 310 incidents through all seven kandas, from Narada's story of the perfect man to Rama's last journey. Day one is the day you install it.
• The wallpaper changes by itself after midnight and keeps working after your phone restarts.
• Put it on the home screen, the lock screen or both.
• Swipe left or right in the app to read ahead or look back, and set any incident as your wallpaper.
• Hide the story text if you only want the painting.

SIX LANGUAGES
English, Malayalam, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Hindi. The app follows your phone's language, or you can choose one in Settings.

THE PAINTINGS
Raja Ravi Varma and the Ravi Varma Press, the Mewar Ramayana, Pahari, Kangra and Mughal Ramayana folios, and more, all public domain or freely licensed from Wikimedia Commons. Every painting is credited with its artist, licence and a link to its source.

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
All paintings are inside the app, so it works without internet. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Ramayanam is the first in a series. Bhagavatham and Mahabharatham are coming next.
